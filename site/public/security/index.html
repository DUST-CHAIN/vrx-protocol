Security & audits

We invite independent security review and publish our audits — in a form that strengthens, not endangers, the system.

VRX invited independent security researchers to assess the platform and committed publicly to publishing every professional audit we receive, and to sharing a portion of any prize we win with the auditors who help strengthen the project.

First audit — November 2026

An independent security researcher (Vicente Aceituno) performed a black-box assessment of the VRX platform and reported a set of findings spanning the web application and the on-chain contract. We treated it as a serious backlog, not noise.

Outcome: every reported finding was remediated, and the web-application fixes are deployed live. The remaining on-chain fixes are implemented and tested in the contract source and ship with our next contract migration. We verified the remediations against the live target. A small number of lower-risk hardening items remain queued as defence-in-depth.

We're grateful for the review — exactly the kind of rigorous, independent scrutiny a privacy-first reservation system should welcome — and we've invited a follow-up assessment.

Our standing posture

  • Privacy is the design. Personal data, prices and deal terms are never placed on-chain; the chain records only that a reservation happened and who holds it. The confidential half stays off-chain.
  • We publish our audits (in a form that strengthens, not endangers, the system) and keep the detailed findings and fixes private so they can't serve as a map for an attacker.
  • We welcome more review. Security researchers: contact us at vrx@vettedrealty.com.
