VRX · How we prove the system works

The VRX Live Control Center.

Before anyone trusts VRX with a real reservation, we have to know every flow actually works — not just that the tests pass, but that the real app does the right thing when real people use it at the same time. Proof of Flows is how. It runs many users at once — each in their real role — straight through the live product, and shows every action and its result on one screen. The recording below is one real, complete, narrated run: 24 flows end to end, including a live AI negotiation.

Buyer Developer Agent Operator
What you’re looking at. Each card on the left is one live user doing their job through the real VRX app. The log on the right is every action they took and what the system did back — a reservation held, a deposit paid into escrow, an attack refused. It runs against the live chain (the block number ticks up as NEAR moves), and it’s deliberately careful with cost — most of it runs for free.

A buyer reserves & pays

Browse → open a private desk chat → reserve a unit → pay the deposit into escrow → see it in “My purchases”. The real end-to-end journey.

The money is protected

The deposit is held in escrow — not the developer’s wallet — and the buyer can pull it back (a full refund) before release. Trust, shown.

Attacks are refused

A stranger trying to pay for someone else, or reserve a unit never offered to them, is turned away. The guards hold.

No double-selling

Two buyers race for the same unit at the same instant — exactly one wins, the other is cleanly told it’s taken. (This run caught and fixed a real bug.)

Live AI negotiation

A real buyer talks to the real sales desk — no script. It negotiates from its true levers, never invents a term or leaks a commission, and closes the deal on camera.
