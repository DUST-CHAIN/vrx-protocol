Section 1

What VRX is

VRX lets an operator with unique, non-substitutable inventory feed live availability to many mutually-distrusting sales teams, and let them browse, book, reserve, pay a deposit and hold — privately.

The one-liner

It is a callable NEAR smart contract (the reservation and hold registry) plus an off-chain portal (the apps each participant uses). Real estate is the beachhead and proof; the primitive is domain-agnostic — unit 312 is not unit 311.

The privacy moat

No personal data, no prices, and no deal terms ever go on-chain. Every real-world identifier is stored as an opaque SHA-256 hash. The chain proves that a reservation exists and who holds it; the what / how-much / with-whom stays off-chain.
