# VRX — daily build log

*A day-by-day record of what was built, drawn from the project's full commit history. The public repository is a
single curated snapshot (squashed for privacy — see the Provenance note in the README), so this log is the honest
timeline that the snapshot alone can't show.*

## The work at a glance

- **Span:** 25 September → 19 November 2026 (~8 weeks)
- **Commits:** 1,307 across 42 active days
- **Peak:** 100 commits in a single day (1 November); routinely 40–90/day through the main stretch
- **Shape:** a steady build from the on-chain primitive outward to the full role-based product, the AI sales desk,
  the authoring studio (Model Maker), and the durability/privacy layer.

> **On the hackathon window.** The official NEAR "Build Beyond Boundaries" build window is **1–14 November 2026**.
> The hackathon rules **explicitly allow prior work and do not judge the commit timeline** — so this log shows the
> *full* arc honestly, with the earlier weeks as foundation and the window clearly marked below. The bulk of the
> product — the live V7 on-chain deploy, the buyer reservation flow, the AI sales desk, Model Maker, and the
> durability layer — was built **inside** the official window.
